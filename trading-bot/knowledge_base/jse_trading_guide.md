# JSE Trading Guide — South African Market Knowledge

## Market Hours
- Pre-market: 08:30 - 09:00 SAST
- Continuous trading: 09:00 - 17:00 SAST
- Closing auction: 16:50 - 17:00 SAST

## Key Indices
- JSE All Share (J203): Broad market index
- JSE Top 40 (J200): 40 largest companies
- JSE Resources (J210): Mining and resources
- JSE Financials (J212): Banks and financial services
- JSE Industrials (J211): Industrial companies

## Sector Breakdown

### Resources (Gold, Platinum, Diversified Mining)
- **Key stocks**: ANG (AngloGold), BHP, GFI (Gold Fields), IMP (Impala Platinum)
- **Drivers**: Commodity prices (gold, PGMs), ZAR/USD exchange rate
- **Edge**: ZAR weakness = higher ZAR revenue for exporters

### Financials (Banks, Insurance)
- **Key stocks**: SBK (Standard Bank), FSR (FirstRand), ABG (Absa), NED (Nedbank)
- **Drivers**: SARB interest rates, credit growth, economic activity
- **Edge**: Rate hikes = wider net interest margins = bank profits

### Consumer (Retail, Food)
- **Key stocks**: SHP (Shoprite), WHL (Woolworths), TFG (The Foschini Group)
- **Drivers**: Consumer confidence, inflation (CPI), employment
- **Edge**: Defensive during uncertainty, but load shedding hurts

### Technology/Media
- **Key stocks**: NPN (Naspers), PRX (Prosus), MTN
- **Drivers**: Tencent performance (for NPN/PRX), data revenue (MTN)
- **Edge**: NPN/PRX trade at massive NAV discount

## Value Screening Criteria for JSE
- P/E ratio <= 20 (JSE average ~15)
- Dividend yield >= 2% (tax-free in TFSA)
- ROE >= 15%
- Debt/equity < 1.5
- Market cap > R10B (liquidity requirement)

## VCP Pattern on JSE Stocks
The Volatility Contraction Pattern works well on JSE because:
1. Lower liquidity means breakouts are more significant
2. Institutional buying creates clear volume signatures
3. SA stocks often base for weeks before major moves
4. Works best on Top 40 constituents (sufficient volume)

### VCP Detection Rules
1. Stock must be in Stage 2 uptrend (above rising 200-day MA)
2. Look for 3-5 progressively tighter price consolidations
3. Each consolidation should have declining volume
4. Breakout should be on 1.5x+ average volume
5. Entry: Buy on breakout above the pattern high
6. Stop: Below the last consolidation low (typically 5-8%)

## Economic Calendar Impact on JSE

### SARB MPC Meetings (every 2 months)
- Rate hike: Banks rally, consumers/retailers sell off, ZAR strengthens
- Rate cut: Consumer stocks rally, banks mixed, ZAR weakens
- Hold: Minimal impact, market focuses on forward guidance

### South African CPI (monthly)
- Above expectations: Hawkish SARB, negative for growth stocks
- Below expectations: Dovish SARB, positive for rate-sensitive stocks

### US Fed Decisions
- Global risk sentiment flows directly to JSE
- Rate hikes = capital outflows from EM including SA
- ZAR typically weakens on hawkish Fed

### Load Shedding
- Stage escalations = negative for consumer, industrial, small caps
- Positive for: Generator companies, solar, battery storage
- Resources less affected (self-generation)

## Risk Management for JSE
- Wider stops needed (lower liquidity = wider spreads)
- Avoid trading around MPC meetings unless positioned before
- TFSA accounts: Tax-free gains, ideal for dividend stocks
- Beware exchange control regulations for offshore moves
