# Market Regime Framework

## Regime Classification (Claude Trading Skills Methodology)

### 1. CONCENTRATION
- **Characteristics**: Few mega-cap leaders drive index returns. Breadth narrowing.
- **Indicators**: RSP/SPY divergence (equal-weight underperforming), top 10 stocks > 30% of index.
- **Strategy**: Focus on leaders only. Reduce position count. Increase concentration in winners.
- **Risk**: Fragile — if leaders crack, index falls fast. Keep tight stops.
- **JSE Example**: When NPN (Naspers/Prosus) dominates JSE Top 40 moves.

### 2. BROADENING
- **Characteristics**: Wide market participation. Many sectors rising. Healthy internals.
- **Indicators**: Advance/decline line rising, new highs expanding, sector breadth > 70%.
- **Strategy**: Maximum exposure allowed. Diversify across strategies. Add positions aggressively.
- **Risk**: Lowest risk regime. Be fully invested within risk limits.
- **JSE Example**: Post-COVID recovery 2020-2021, broad JSE participation.

### 3. CONTRACTION
- **Characteristics**: Declining breadth. Defensive rotation. Risk-off sentiment.
- **Indicators**: New lows expanding, distribution days accumulating, credit spreads widening.
- **Strategy**: REDUCE EXPOSURE. Close weak positions. No new entries. Raise cash.
- **Risk**: High — capital preservation mode. Move to 50%+ cash.
- **JSE Example**: Load shedding escalation periods, political instability.

### 4. INFLATIONARY
- **Characteristics**: Rising commodities, falling bonds, rotation to energy/materials/resources.
- **Indicators**: CPI rising, bond yields rising, commodity indices breaking out.
- **Strategy**: Overweight resources, gold miners. Underweight growth/tech. Crypto may benefit.
- **Risk**: SARB rate hikes impact consumer stocks and banks differently.
- **JSE Example**: Gold miners (ANG, GFI) outperforming when ZAR weakens.

### 5. TRANSITIONAL
- **Characteristics**: Mixed signals. Regime changing. Unclear direction.
- **Indicators**: Conflicting breadth signals, sector rotation unclear, volatility elevated.
- **Strategy**: REDUCE SIZE on all trades. Wait for clarity. Focus on pair trades (market neutral).
- **Risk**: Whipsaw risk high. False breakouts common.

## Exposure Recommendations by Regime

| Regime | Max Equity Exposure | New Entries | Stop Tightness |
|--------|-------------------|-------------|----------------|
| Broadening | 80-100% | Aggressive | Normal |
| Concentration | 50-70% | Selective | Tight |
| Transitional | 30-50% | Cautious | Tight |
| Inflationary | 40-60% | Sector-specific | Normal |
| Contraction | 0-30% | NONE | Very tight |

## South African Market Specifics

- SARB rate decisions heavily impact all JSE sectors
- ZAR weakness = positive for resource exporters (ANG, BHP, GFI)
- ZAR weakness = negative for retailers (SHP, WHL, TFG) and banks
- Load shedding stages directly correlate with market sentiment
- Dual-listed stocks (BHP, GLN) respond to both local and global factors
