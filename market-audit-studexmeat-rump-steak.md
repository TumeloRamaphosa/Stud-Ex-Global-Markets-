## 3. CRITICAL ISSUES (Fix Immediately)

### 3.1 Pricing Error on 500g Variant
The 500g variant shows a "regular" price of **R68.90** crossed out with a sale price of **R450**. This is clearly inverted — the sale price is higher than the regular price. This **destroys trust instantly** and signals carelessness to premium buyers.

**Fix:** Correct the 500g regular price (likely should be R629 or similar to maintain the ~28% discount structure matching the 1kg variant).

### 3.2 Only 1 Review = Zero Social Proof
A single 5-star review on a premium product (R450–R850) is worse than no reviews at all. Premium buyers need validation before spending.

**Fix:**
- Launch a post-purchase email sequence requesting reviews (offer 10% off next order)
- Add photo reviews (tools: Judge.me, Loox, or Stamped.io for Shopify)
- Display review count prominently — aim for 15+ reviews within 60 days
- Add a "Customer Photos" section below the product

### 3.3 No Cooking/Preparation Guidance
You're selling a premium cut that many buyers may not know how to prepare properly. A bad cooking experience = no repeat purchase.

**Fix:**
- Add a "How to Cook Wagyu Rump Steak" tab/section with:
  - Recommended internal temps
  - Pan-sear vs. braai instructions
  - Resting time guidance
  - Serving suggestions with photos
- Embed a short (60-90s) cooking video

The page says "limited time offer" but has no countdown timer, no stock indicator, and no deadline. Vague urgency doesn't convert.

### 4.2 Missing Nutritional Information
Health-conscious premium buyers (your target market) want macros — protein, fat content, calories per serving. This is table stakes for premium meat e-commerce.
