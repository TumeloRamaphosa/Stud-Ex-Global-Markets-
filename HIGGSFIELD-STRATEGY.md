# Higgsfield.ai Strategy Guide for Stud-Ex

## Content Formats Available

| Format | Best For | Platform |
|--------|----------|----------|
| UGC | Authentic product recommendations, trust-building | IG Reels, TikTok, Facebook |
| TV Spot | Brand awareness, cinematic storytelling | Facebook Ads, YouTube |
| Tutorial | Demonstrating how services work | Instagram, YouTube Shorts |
| Product Review | Hands-on demos, close-up showcases | IG Reels, TikTok |
| Unboxing | Launch announcements, reveals | TikTok, IG Stories |
| Hyper Motion (CGI) | Premium brand positioning, no-people product hero | LinkedIn, Facebook Ads |
| Wild Card | Creative campaigns with full AI direction | Any platform |

## 4-3-2 Content Mix Rule (per 9 posts)
- 4 posts: educate/entertain (no selling)
- 3 posts: showcase work/results/process
- 2 posts: directly promote service/offer

## Weekly Calendar
- Monday: Educational (Tutorial format)
- Wednesday: Product showcase (UGC/Product Review)
- Friday: Behind-the-scenes (Wild Card)
- Saturday: Promotional (TV Spot/Hyper Motion)
- Daily Stories: Quick tips, polls, Q&A

## SA Market Benchmarks (2025-2026)
- Instagram (1K-10K): 3.5% avg, 5-8% good, 10%+ excellent
- Facebook Pages: 0.5-1% avg, 2-3% good, 5%+ excellent
- Instagram Reels: 4-6% avg, 8-12% good, 15%+ excellent
